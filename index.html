Why CAREWEAVE

Because recovery should never feel invisible.

Postpartum care continues after discharge. CAREWEAVE brings selected physiological signals into one understandable experience for the mother and her support network.

♥

Real-time monitoring

Live heart rate, temperature and respiration readings are presented without overwhelming the user.

◒

Early risk detection

An explainable prototype engine combines changes across readings to surface warning levels.

🔔

People stay informed

Alerts can be made visible to caregivers, with emergency actions available for severe situations.

⌁

Offline-first prototype

Readings and alerts can remain available locally when connectivity is unreliable.

Designed for real life

Technology that cares, so the family can too.

The experience is intentionally calm: simple numbers, trend views and clear risk states instead of complicated medical interfaces.

✓Mother-first: comfortable wearable concept with detachable electronics.
✓Caregiver-friendly: important changes are surfaced clearly rather than buried in charts.
✓Action-oriented: LOW, MODERATE and HIGH states explain what the prototype is seeing.
✓Connected when possible: ESP32 + BLE provides the hardware communication path.
Inside the weave

Advanced care, woven into every thread.

The concept pairs textile-integrated sensing with a small detachable electronics module. The digital layer then turns incoming measurements into a simple monitoring experience.

Safety first

Transparent about what the prototype can — and cannot — do.

CAREWEAVE is positioned as a monitoring and early-warning system. Clinical validation and regulatory work are required before any clinical use.
